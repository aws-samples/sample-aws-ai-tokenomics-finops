# AI Tokenomics FinOps

A self-assessment runbook for AI token cost on AWS. Teams use it to find the decisions that move their token bill.

## Language

**Runbook**:
The published guidance: one static, browser-only page, available as the live site or as the self-contained `content/index.html`.
_Avoid_: tool, app, site (when meaning the product)

**Track**:
One of the two audiences the runbook serves: AI Coding Assistant (Track 1) or Purpose-Built Agentic AI Application (Track 2).
_Avoid_: path, mode, persona

**Actor layer**:
A grouping of levers within a track by who controls them: Model Inference, Agent Harness, End User.
_Avoid_: category, pillar

**Lever**:
One decision that moves the bill, with its diagnostic questions and detailed guidance.
_Avoid_: tip, recommendation, best practice

**Diagnostic question**:
A question under a lever, answered Fully covered, Partially, Not covered, or N/A.
_Avoid_: check, control

**Assessment**:
One pass by a team through a track's diagnostic questions.
_Avoid_: audit, review, survey

**Gap report**:
The dated output of an assessment: coverage counts, the gaps with links to guidance, and every answer with notes. Exported as Markdown or PDF.
_Avoid_: results, output, scorecard
